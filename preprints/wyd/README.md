> **Abstract.** Gibilisco and Isola conjectured that the scalar curvature of the Wigner–Yanase–Dyson metric with parameter $`p`$ on the invertible density matrices increases under mixing for all $`p`$ in some interval $`(1,1+\varepsilon)`$; the case $`p\downarrow1`$ is Petz's conjecture for the Kubo–Mori metric (resolved in a companion paper). We prove that for $`p\in(1-\varepsilon,1+\varepsilon)`$, with $`\varepsilon>0`$ independent of the dimension, the scalar curvature is a concave function of the eigenvalues and hence increases under mixing. By Dittmann's formula it suffices to prove that one symmetric function $`H_p`$ of three positive variables is concave, which we do by showing that $`-1/H_p`$ is a Stieltjes transform along every line in the positive cone, uniformly in $`p`$ near $`1`$. The value of $`\varepsilon`$ is not explicit.
