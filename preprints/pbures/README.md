> **Abstract.** We study the $`p`$-Bures–Wasserstein distance $`D_p(A,B)=(\mathrm{tr}\,A^p+\mathrm{tr}\,B^p-2\|A^{1/2}B^{1/2}\|_p^p)^{1/2}`$ on positive semidefinite matrices; it equals the Bures–Wasserstein distance for $`p=1`$, and the Hilbert–Schmidt distance for $`p=2`$. We prove that $`D_p`$ is a metric for every $`1\le p\le2`$, by proving a stronger 2014 conjecture of Lin and van den Driessche on $`3\times3`$ block matrices. Our main tool is a partition inequality for Schatten norms, proved by complex interpolation. We also characterize Fréchet means for $`D_p`$ by a fixed-point equation and prove geometric convergence of the associated iteration.
