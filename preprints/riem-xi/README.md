## v2 — 29 October 2026

**On some positivity properties of the Riemann $\Xi$ function** · 27 pages

| File | Timestamp |
|---|---|
| [`riem-xi-v2.pdf`](v2/riem-xi-v2.pdf) | [`riem-xi-v2.pdf.ots`](v2/riem-xi-v2.pdf.ots) |

> **Abstract.** We prove strict log-concavity of the positive functions $`\Theta`$ and $`\Lambda`$ with one-sided and bilateral Laplace transforms $`1/\xi(\tfrac12+\sqrt s)`$ and $`1/\Xi`$, respectively, where $`\Xi(z)=\xi(\tfrac12+iz)`$. For either function, log-concavity is the Pólya frequency property of order two (PF$`_2`$). Without assuming RH, but using known zero verification through height $`3\cdot10^{12}`$, we further prove that $`\Lambda`$ is a PF function of order $`256`$ (PF$`_{256}`$). We also prove local PF positivity for $`\Theta`$: for each fixed order, the ordered minors of $`\Theta(x-y)`$ are positive when all differences $`x_i-y_j`$ are sufficiently small and positive. Additionally, we prove the generalized Laguerre inequalities: if RH holds up to height $`T>1/2`$, then $`L_n[\Xi](x)\ge0`$ for every $`n\ge0`$ and $`|x|\le T-1/2`$; the case $`n=1`$ is due to Csordas, Ruttan and Varga (1991).
