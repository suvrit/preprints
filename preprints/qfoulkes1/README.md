> **Abstract.** We prove that the plethysm $`H_m[\widetilde H_\mu]`$ of a Macdonald polynomial by the Hall–Littlewood polynomial $`H_m`$ lies above the Macdonald polynomial $`\widetilde H_{m\mu}`$ obtained by fusing $`m`$ copies of $`\widetilde H_\mu`$. More specifically, the quotient $`(H_m[\widetilde H_\mu]-\widetilde H_{m\mu})/(1-q^m)`$ is Schur positive with coefficients in $`\mathbb N[q,t]`$, and it is the character of an explicit module that we call the fusion defect. For one-row shapes the inequality places both $`q`$-Foulkes plethysms $`H_b[H_a]`$ and $`H_a[H_b]`$ above the same polynomial $`H_{ab}`$, and comparing their two defects we obtain a distorted version of Bergeron's $`q`$-analog of Foulkes' conjecture, in which the subtracted plethysm is shifted by $`q^{a(a-1)\lfloor b/2\rfloor}`$.
