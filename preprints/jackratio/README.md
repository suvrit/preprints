> **Abstract.** We study the inequality $`P_\lambda(x;q,t)/P_\lambda(1^n;q,t)\geq P_\mu(x;q,t)/P_\mu(1^n;q,t)`$ for all $`x\in\mathbb{R}_{>0}^n`$ whenever the partition $`\lambda`$ majorizes $`\mu`$; Sra suggested it in 2015, and Chen, Khare and Sahi stated it as a conjecture in 2025. We prove the Jack case for every $`n`$ from Sun's positive Gelfand–Tsetlin integral and the Dixon–Anderson recursion. For Macdonald polynomials we prove the case $`n=2`$, the covers $`(d)\succeq(d-1,1)`$ for every $`n`$ when $`t\leq q`$, and, by exact certificates, the case $`n=3`$ with $`|\lambda|\leq 8`$. The HCIZ mechanism behind the Jack proof has no direct finite-$`q`$ analogue: at two explicit pairs $`(q,t)`$, no positive representation of the normalized ratio has a kernel multiplicative in $`\lambda`$, and normalizing at the principal specialization $`t^\delta`$ instead fails the inequality.
