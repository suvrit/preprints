> **Abstract.** We prove that the mixed determinant $`\eta(\cdot)`$ of Borcea and Brändén has a spectrahedral hyperbolicity cone. More precisely, for Hermitian matrices $`A_1,\ldots,A_m`$ of order $`d`$, the hyperbolicity cone of $`\eta(A_1,\ldots,A_m)`$ is cut out by $`\sum_j\Phi_j(A_j)\succeq0`$, where $`\Phi_1,\ldots,\Phi_m`$ are explicit unital completely positive maps. We argue by induction on $`d`$, adding one row and one column to every argument and expressing the logarithmic derivatives of $`\eta`$ as extremal eigenvalues. By compressing along polynomial boundary kernels, we reduce the order of the pencil $`\sum_j\Phi_j(A_j)`$ to $`\exp(O(md^3\log m))`$; for one fixed positive semidefinite $`n\times n`$ matrix $`A`$ a separate pencil yields the order $`\exp(O(n^2\log n))`$. We also answer four problems of Borcea and Brändén negatively.
