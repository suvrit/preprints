> **Abstract.** Bergeron's $`q`$-analog of Foulkes' conjecture asserts that $`(H_b[H_a]-H_a[H_b])/(1-q)`$ is Schur positive for $`a\le b`$, where $`H_n`$ is the (modified) Hall--Littlewood polynomial. We view this conjecture probabilistically: on each irreducible representation of $`S_{ab}`$, the two plethysms induce degree distributions, and positivity means that the first is stochastically smaller than the second. We prove that, for a random (Plancherel) irreducible representation and a degree sampled from its graded multiplicity, the corresponding coefficient is negative with probability at most $`C\exp(-c(ab)^{1/3})`$, uniformly in $`a\le b`$. Consequently, the $`q`$-Foulkes conjecture holds after deleting a fraction at most $`C\exp(-c(ab)^{1/3})`$ of each of the two graded modules. Additionally, we show that exact conditional means and variances yield complete positivity for explicit width and height families.
