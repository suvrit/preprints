> **Abstract.** We prove that the Hankel kernel $`\Phi(\sqrt{x+y})`$, where $`\Phi`$ is the Jacobi theta kernel whose cosine transform is the Riemann $`\Xi`$-function, is strictly reverse-regular through order four. The order is sharp: an interval enclosure of the fifth Hankel–Wronskian at $`t=10^{-2}`$ shows failure at order five. The third-order condition is Csordas's conjecture that $`s'^2-ss''`$ is strictly log-concave for $`s(t)=\Phi(\sqrt t)`$. The proof reduces the kernel minors to Wronskian signs and establishes the signs by exact polynomial estimates for $`t\ge1`$ and interval arithmetic on $`[0,1]`$. The fourth-order sign also yields three positive iterates of $`s\mapsto s'^2-ss''`$, the triple Turán inequalities for the normalized Taylor coefficients of $`\Xi`$, and quartic integral inequalities for $`\Xi`$.
