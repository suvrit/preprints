> **Abstract.** We introduce the Macdonald $`(q,t)`$-permanent, a two-parameter cycle-weighted permanent that contains the usual permanent and determinant, as well as the $`\alpha`$-permanent of Vere-Jones, as degenerate cases. We prove a MacMahon-type master theorem for its generating function, and determine when it induces a valid kernel on tuples of vectors. Nonnegativity of the $`(q,t)`$-permanent itself on semidefinite matrices turns out to be subtler: for $`t=q^\alpha`$ nonnegativity holds exactly at the positive Wallach exponents; for a fixed $`q`$ we determine the region in rank two and for higher ranks under an additional condition, while for small $`q`$ positivity can even reach beyond the kernel region. Whether positivity forces $`t\in\{0,q,q^2,\ldots\}`$ remains an intriguing open problem.
