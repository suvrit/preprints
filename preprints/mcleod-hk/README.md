> **Abstract.** We prove McLeod's (1959) conjecture that $`(h_{\ell+r}/h_\ell)^{1/r}`$ is subadditive for all $`\ell\ge0`$ and $`r\ge1`$, where $`h_k`$ is the complete homogeneous symmetric polynomial of degree $`k`$ (J. B. McLeod, "On four inequalities in symmetric functions", Proc. Edinburgh Math. Soc., 1959). We first prove that the ratio $`h_k/h_{k-1}`$ is convex, and our proof takes an unexpected detour through queuing theory. The geometric mean of $`r`$ consecutive such ratios equals McLeod's conjectured function, but geometric means of convex functions need not be convex. Our second and decisive step is a tensorization trick that couples neighboring ratios tightly enough to make their geometric mean convex.
