> **Abstract.** We prove a sharp Brégman–Minc bound for permanents of nonnegative matrices that was conjectured by Samorodnitsky and Gurvits. Specifically, for $`\phi(x)=\Gamma(1+1/x)^{-x}`$, with $`\phi(0)=0`$, applied entrywise, we show that every nonnegative row-stochastic square matrix $`A`$ satisfies $`\operatorname{per}\phi[A]\le1`$, which is the Brégman–Minc inequality when each row of $`A`$ is uniform on its support. The crux of our proof is a scalar inequality on the simplex, which we prove using a carefully chosen yet elementary integral representation. We note brief applications to the entropy of a random permutation and to sharp bounds for rectangular permanents and matchings.
